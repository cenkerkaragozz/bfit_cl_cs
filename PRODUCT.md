# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

BrainFit Karşıyaka serves two equal public audiences through separate landing pages. The main landing page is for parents who are worried about a child's attention, memory, learning rhythm, homework habits, or school performance, and who need a calm way to decide whether Zihin Check-Up is the right first step. The `/yetiskinler` page is for adults seeking cognitive performance support around focus, memory, learning capacity, mental clarity, exam performance, work performance, or active aging.

## Product Purpose

The site explains BrainFit Karşıyaka's Zihin Check-Up and cognitive development programs, helps visitors understand which support path fits their situation, and converts qualified visitors into consultation or check-up requests. Success means parents and adults feel informed, not pressured, and complete the correct appointment form with enough context for the team to follow up.

## Brand Personality

Calm, credible, encouraging. The voice should feel expert without becoming cold, supportive without becoming sentimental, and practical without reducing the topic to generic self-improvement copy. Copy should respect family concerns and adult performance goals while avoiding diagnosis language, guarantees, or miracle claims.

## Anti-references

Avoid sterile hospital aesthetics, childish edtech visuals, aggressive sales funnels, fear-based parenting copy, miracle-brain or IQ-boost claims, generic wellness-coach language, and template landing-page patterns that rely on repeated icon cards, exaggerated metrics, or vague transformation promises.

## Design Principles

- Lead with reassurance, then evidence: visitors should feel understood before being asked to act.
- Separate support from diagnosis: explain what BrainFit can assess and support without implying medical treatment.
- Preserve two audience paths: parents and adults share the brand, but each page should speak to its own context and goals.
- Make the next step concrete: every section should clarify whether the visitor should learn more, choose a concern, or request a check-up.
- Keep credibility warm: use expert framing, local trust, and clear boundaries without losing the encouraging tone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Body text, form labels, buttons, badges, and placeholder text should meet contrast requirements. Motion should respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, interactive controls need visible focus states, and forms should remain understandable with clear labels, status messages, and touch-friendly targets.
